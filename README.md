# Privacy Policy for Animal Puzzle Saga

**Effective Date:** September 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 21, 2026  

Welcome to **Animal Puzzle Saga** ("the App"), developed by **Yassin Masrouri** ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Animal Puzzle Saga does **not** require user accounts, logins, email registrations, or personal profile creation.

* **Local Storage:** The App stores game progression locally on your device (completed levels, star ratings, and audio settings) using standard web storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device and is not collected or transmitted to any external server.
* **Device Permissions:** The App only requests the `INTERNET` permission to retrieve high-resolution puzzle imagery and deliver advertisement content.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements
The App may use third-party service providers that collect information used to identify your device:

* **Google AdMob & Google Play Services:** We may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), approximate IP location, and device metrics to serve relevant or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
* **Unsplash:** The App streams wildlife puzzle photography via Unsplash. No personal data is shared with or collected by the image content delivery network.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:  
[Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

---

### 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
The App is designed as a casual brain-training puzzle game suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

---

### 4. Data Security
Because we do not operate external databases holding personal user data, your game data remains entirely on your local device. Uninstalling the App removes all locally saved progress.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications become effective immediately upon posting the updated policy online.
